Start Easy, Grow Faster with Nivesh Mitra

  • Core Features

    • Completely Online - End to End Online Application
    • Know Your Approvals & Incentives
    • One Time Common information to be Submitted through CAF
    • Consolidated Fee Payment
    • Third Party Verification
    • Land Bank (GIS) Information

  • Key Features

    • Single-Window Clearance : Multiple approvals through one portal.
    • Track in Real-Time : Get instant updates on your applications.
    • Paperless Process : Submit and verify documents digitally.

  • Advance Features

    • Fast Approvals : Accelerated processing for quicker business setup
    • Instant Notifications : Get alerts for every update or action required
    • Integrated Services : Connect with multiple departments seamlessly

  • What makes your business plans so special?

    • Regulatory Compliance : Adheres to all state and central laws
    • Mandatory Approvals : Secures licenses and permits required by the government
    • Timely Updates : Keeps businesses informed about policy changes
